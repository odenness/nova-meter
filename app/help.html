NOVAMETER

A read-only combat meter for Aion 2. It watches the game's network traffic and shows your party's damage, healing and boss fights. It never touches the game, its files or its memory.

One more step: install Npcap

Nova Meter reads game traffic through Npcap, a free, widely used Windows capture driver (Wireshark uses it too). It can't be bundled, so please install it once:
  1. Click Download Npcap and run the installer.
  2. Keep the default options. Leave "Restrict Npcap driver's access to Administrators only" unticked.
  3. Come back here and click I've installed it.

✔ Npcap is installed. Nova Meter is capturing. You can close this window.

Getting started

  1. Start Nova Meter before you log in or change zone. The game only says who everyone is when they first appear, so this is how party names and boss names show up straight away.
  2. Play Aion 2 in borderless windowed mode (exclusive fullscreen hides overlays).
  3. Drag the meter by its title bar, resize from the bottom-right corner. Position and size are remembered.
  4. Click any player for their breakdown: skills, hits, defense and rotation.

Hotkeys

Ctrl + Shift + LLock / unlock. Locked = clicks go straight through to the game.
Ctrl + Shift + HHide / show the overlay.

What the numbers mean

DPSDamage per second over the fight.
HPSHealing on other players per second (self-heals and overhealing left out).
DTPSDamage taken per second.
ShareYour part of the party's total.
Current / OverallThis fight, or every fight since Nova Meter started.
Boss barHP left and the time to kill at your party's current pace.
History⋯ → History. Every fight is saved on your PC. Open one for the full report, or copy a summary for game chat.
Personal bestsBeat your best DPS on a boss and you get a celebration. Turn it off in Settings.

Troubleshooting

Meter stays emptyCheck Npcap is installed (top of this page), then restart Nova Meter. VPNs or routing tools that tunnel game traffic can hide it.
Players show as #1234Nova Meter started after they appeared. Change zone or re-enter the dungeon and names fill in.
"Unknown boss"The boss was already there before Nova Meter started. Next time start it before zoning in. You can also click a boss name to name it yourself.
"Windows protected your PC"The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.
Can't click the meterIt's locked. Press Ctrl + Shift + L.

Privacy

Everything stays on your PC (%APPDATA%\NovaMeter). The only thing Nova Meter sends anywhere is an enemy's ID number, once, to the public gamers4.life Aion 2 database to look up its name. No account, no telemetry.

Found a bug?

Click the bug icon in the meter's footer (or ⋯ → Report a bug), or open an issue on GitHub. A screenshot and the boss/zone help a lot.
